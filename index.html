About Us

Welcome to CAVU Flight Training. As a dedicated flight instructor, my mission is to help you achieve your aviation goals safely and efficiently, whether you're taking your very first discovery flight or looking to add new ratings to your license.

We specialize in Single-Engine Airplanes and Gliders. To provide flexibility and convenience, we actively fly out of several premier airports in Maine:

  • Portland (PWM)
  • Lewiston (LEW)
  • Brunswick (BXM)
  • Biddeford (SFM)
  • Sanford (SFM)
